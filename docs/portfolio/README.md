## The one-paragraph version

A third-year English Literature student at Universitas Indonesia who is really a marketing strategist: he writes the 32-page strategy, runs the campaign, measures it against a baseline he set himself, and when the tool he needs doesn't exist he codes it. The site claims the **strategist** category, proves it with a measured cause-and-effect chain, and treats his five competition placings as the rare assets they are — five pages, not five bullets.

The design direction is **SIGNAL**: the homepage is a tuning instrument — a printed scale card, a glowing blue needle, a band switch, and a signal meter that renders his result against a labelled noise floor. You tune to find a project. Station changes arrive through a burst of static and masked line-swaps; opening one grows a sheet of paper out of the artifact card's real on-screen rectangle. Built to the craft rules extracted from four reference builds, on hand-coded static HTML. Mobile-first, because the audience will read it on a phone on 4G.

## Decisions locked

- **Goal:** hire-first, with a credible path for consulting work
- **Stack:** hand-coded static HTML/CSS/JS, 80-line Node templating step, GitHub Pages
- **Direction:** **SIGNAL** — a tuning instrument; blue backlight, printed scale card, signal meter
- **Positioning line:** *"I don't stop at the deck."*
- **Palette:** `#F2EFE8` paper · `#101A24` blue-black ink · `#070D14` stage · `#2B5CF0` blue · `#5BE1FF` glow
- **Type:** Newsreader (serif) · Inter Tight (grotesque) · Geist Mono (data)
- **Motion law:** masks and clips only — **zero opacity fades**
- **Content:** twelve placeholder stations ship day one; real content swaps in later
