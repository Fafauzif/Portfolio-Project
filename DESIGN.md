# Portfolio design notes

The home question is printed on a navy sheet. The first movement tips the question mark and raises the corner as the reader starts to scroll. The sheet folds and the campaign print on its reverse settles into the first project cover. The opening keeps the navigation, question, and working link to Selected Work. The link leaves with the opening instead of crossing the poster.

The work archive separates campaigns, building, proposals, and editorial work through type and spacing. Selected Work gives the opening a concrete answer. The Lab lets visitors inspect prints and experiments separately from the case studies. Experience and recognition connect back to the records that support them.

Navy supplies the dark sheet and some case openings. Pale paper supports long reading and light blue emphasizes interaction. Newsreader carries the display type, Inter Tight carries copy, and Geist Mono supplies secondary facts. Display size follows the space available to the text. Complete names and phrases are the primary units of composition. Long titles can use extra lines with readable spacing, and supporting information has its own room.

| Interaction | Design intent |
| --- | --- |
| Printed question and fold | A short response starts the native-scroll transition. The card lands once and can rewind. |
| Compact navigation | On Home, the rail follows the fold from dark to light while keeping links legible. Interior links retain a stable position and clear focus. |
| Archive | Search and filter complete case records. Quick View provides a closer look from the list. |
| Case studies | Put title, the scope of work, and role where a reader can scan them. The linked work categories use the visual language of the small squared controls. |
| Lab | Compare projects in a keyboard-friendly list or move and inspect prints on the desk. |
| Pointer response | A soft light sits under the fine pointer and fades at rest. Artwork and controls use light within their own boundaries. |
| About portrait | Fauzi’s photo sits in front of overlapping paper stock. One cobalt corner connects the portrait to the opening fold. |
| Artwork and links | A small settling movement emphasizes selected artwork. Link arrows give short directional feedback. Reading stays steady. |
| Contact | The contact content fills the available page height before handing off to the footer. Longer content can flow beyond that height. |
| Reduced motion | The question, project links, and essential content are available in their resting states. |

The unified variations of hover-border lighting and local pointer following were informed by [Hover Border Gradient](https://ui.aceternity.com/components/hover-border-gradient) and [Following Pointer](https://ui.aceternity.com/components/following-pointer).
