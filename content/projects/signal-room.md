## A notice without a home

The challenge asked teams to write a communications plan for a fictional arts venue closing temporarily. Prior examples used several channels with slightly different information. We set out to make the authoritative update easy to recognize and pass along.

## Give every channel a job

The team compared app notifications, posters, email, and a simple status page. We designed a public notice that always points to one address, with a short dated update on the page and copies in the window, ticket email, and listing. The same plain language can be read out by someone on the phone.

I wrote the notice, created the sequence of messages, and explained the choices in the pitch. Another teammate handled production examples.

## The useful question from the panel

A judge asked who updates the information after hours. We added an owner and an expiry to the handoff plan before final submission. The pitch received a special mention. Its most useful artifact is a publishing checklist the venue could use to maintain a single version across channels.
