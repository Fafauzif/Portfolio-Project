## Start with one sheet

I wanted to see whether ordinary paper could hold attention for a whole shot. The first version had multiple pieces and a camera move. Every part competed to explain what was happening. I pared it down to one fold, one camera, and a pool of directional light.

## Make the crease carry the motion

I modeled the sheet in two surfaces with a common edge and built the fold around that edge. A continuous stroke crosses the joint, making it easy to see when the paper changes angle. I tested roughness and light placement so the highlight remained visible without making the material look like plastic.

My work covered the object, composition, material, and final image. I used an assistant during iteration to check camera values and compare lighting options.

## What the still can tell you

I saved the folded resting state, the midpoint, and the open state as references of the geometry. At each stage the edge should make sense without motion. The intended motion is a single lift and settling fold; the sequence has a clear final pose so a gallery can pause at any time.
