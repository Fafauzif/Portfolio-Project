## Visibility is part of access

Regional languages have a place in Indonesian cultural policy. Our team looked at a different setting: the online platforms where people discover and circulate language every day. We asked what regional-language creators and audiences need if these spaces are to offer them a fair opportunity to be found.

## Measure a disparity carefully

The team compared a sample of public TikTok posts and gathered survey responses to explore perceptions of language visibility. The available sample showed a view-count disparity. A platform feed is shaped by many factors, so the finding informed the proposal as an observed association.

## Build a phased proposal

We proposed coordination between relevant institutions, investment in regional-language digital infrastructure, and phased accountability for platform reporting. Each part had a different purpose: clarify who is responsible, improve how local-language content can be recognized, and give reviewers a way to check progress over time.

I contributed as a member of the Universitas Indonesia team. The proposal received a Meritorious Proposal distinction at the 2026 finals in China. Its recommendations were submitted as policy design work.
