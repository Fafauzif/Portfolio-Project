## A link people could share

The [G-Consulting recruitment campaign](/work/g-consulting-mentee-recruitment/) needed a referral path that applicants could pass along and the team could verify. Each valid referral needed to count once and land in the same ledger. The workflow had to be simple enough to operate during recruitment.

## Build the handoff

I designed, coded, installed, and tested the workflow in Google Apps Script. It generated personal codes and prefilled links, sent individualized emails, checked incoming submissions, and grouped valid referrals in an automated ledger. Validation covered self-referrals, repeat applicants, and duplicate referrals.

The ledger supported the program’s benefit rules and made the team’s records easier to reconcile. I left a public leaderboard out of the design, keeping the applicant-facing part small and the validation with the team.

## Separate working software from uptake

The workflow ran during the recruitment cycle. Twenty-two of the 691 applicants used valid codes. That was a modest share of the total. It gave us a concrete question for a later cycle: can someone understand when to use the link and why sharing it is worth the effort?
