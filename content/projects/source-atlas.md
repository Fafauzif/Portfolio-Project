## What vanished when the findings went into slides

In a slide deck, a quote can be hard to trace back to the interview and question that gave it meaning. In [What Sticks](/work/what-sticks/) I followed one note through that process on paper. I designed Source Atlas for the moment a colleague wants to examine the source behind a decision.

The first screen is a short list of decisions. A visitor can open a decision to find the notes, contradictions, and unresolved questions associated with it. A source can support several decisions without creating a second copy.

## Build an index someone can keep

I tested a general tag organizer and a narrow relation between notes and decisions. I took the latter forward. It has less expressive tagging, but gives a small team a predictable place to look when a choice is challenged.

I built the prototype and its data model. I used practice transcripts with conflicting observations to check that the interface could show where the evidence pointed in different directions. Import and export use a plain format so another team can reuse the notes in a different tool.

## Where I would test it next

A short research sprint is a useful pilot. I want to compare whether a researcher can recover the context of one note, and whether a colleague can do the same after the original researcher leaves the project.
