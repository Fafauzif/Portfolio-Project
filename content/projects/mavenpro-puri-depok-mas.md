## The uncertainty before an inquiry

Property marketing can show the right house and still leave a first-time buyer with questions about documents, mortgages, or what happens at a site visit. Through MavenPRO, I led a five-person team to study the communications around that decision and propose a more useful path to an inquiry.

## Start with the buyer’s questions

We reviewed the brand’s existing Instagram and website, surveyed 40 respondents, held two exploratory interviews, and benchmarked three competitors. We then mapped what buyers needed to understand as they moved from discovery toward an inquiry or a visit.

Three lines of work followed: give buyers trustworthy proof, explain the steps in a first purchase, and make it easier to ask a question. The mockups included home tours, practical documentation guidance, and a clearer WhatsApp path.

## Deliver the system

I coordinated the workstreams, helped turn the research into a final communications strategy, and presented the 20-page deck to the client. It included eight content or journey mockups, a four-week publishing plan, a phased roadmap, and measures for the proposed inquiry journey.

The work concluded with the presentation. The roadmap and its targets describe the recommended next steps for the client.
