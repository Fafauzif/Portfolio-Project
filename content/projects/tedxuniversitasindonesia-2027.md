## Work across the whole program

The 2027 edition began its organizing cycle in 2026. As Director of Marketing, I coordinated Social Media, External Affairs, and Visual Design. The annual challenge was to help the three divisions plan beyond an individual announcement or event.

## Connect the seven program moments

I wrote the annual marketing plan around a sequence of audience decisions. The earlier programs give someone a small way to encounter the ideas. Other events give that person a reason to take part, return, or bring someone else. By the main event, the audience should already understand what they are choosing to attend.

The plan gave teams shared questions for a brief: what is the audience deciding here, what action are we asking for, and which team owns the next step?

## Review the right evidence

We could review an early event for discovery and return participation, then use different measures when asking for a registration or more substantial commitment. That distinction informed planning and reporting across the program. The annual plan is an operating document, and program activation and follow-through are tracked separately from the proposals in it.
