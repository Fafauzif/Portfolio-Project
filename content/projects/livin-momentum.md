## Look past first use

Our three-person team worked on a case for Livin’ by Mandiri. We examined where the app fits into ordinary money decisions: how someone checks what they can spend, organizes a payment, or chooses an app for a recurring task.

## Ask about a habit

The team used survey responses and other research to consider how customers split financial tasks across banking apps and wallets. An existing customer could use Livin’ and still give another app a larger place in their routine. That distinction prompted us to examine repeated use and the confidence to manage money inside the app.

## Propose the next move

Livin’Momentum organized the recommendations around routine ownership, proactive money management, and digital confidence. The proposal paired the features and communications ideas with a modeled business case. The financial projections were part of the submission and depended on its assumptions.

The team received National 1st Place in the BILLIONS 2026 Mini Case Competition at Universitas Brawijaya.
