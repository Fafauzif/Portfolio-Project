## The shape of the gap

Heat guidance rarely lists specific indoor locations that can serve a resident at that hour. Our team examined an invented city district with libraries, storefronts, and community rooms already serving different groups. I mapped the service constraints and helped make the policy brief practical.

## Match the invitation to an open door

We considered a new network of cooling centers, a public map, and agreements with places residents already knew. The proposal combines the latter two. Each host publishes clear opening hours, seating, water, and accessibility details. A light-touch confirmation process helps the directory describe what is true on the ground.

That choice depends on hosts having time to update information. We included periodic checks by city staff alongside the listing form.

## A limited proposal

I wrote the service map and implementation stages with input from a teammate studying policy and another developing the illustrative scenario. We proposed a short pilot with a subset of hosts, and named outdated hours as a priority measure. The forum selected the brief for its public reading. The proposed evaluation starts with the accuracy of listings and separates that measure from longer-term health outcomes.
